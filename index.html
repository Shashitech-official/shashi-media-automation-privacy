Privacy Policy

Shashi Media Automation — last updated October 9, 2026.

About this app

This is a personal automation used by its owner to create videos and publish them to the owner-managed Instagram account @vibehaze_english. It is not offered to the public for processing other users’ Instagram accounts.

Information processed and use

To publish the owner’s selected content, the workflow may process Instagram and professional-account identifiers, authorization tokens, and the Page or Instagram account association needed for publishing. It processes videos and captions supplied or created by the owner’s workflow. Account authorization is handled through Meta. Published videos and captions are sent to Meta and appear on Instagram according to account settings. Prompts or media may also be sent to video-generation services configured by the owner, which handle them under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and retention

The owner runs n8n on their own system and controls its workflow data, credentials, and retention settings. Meta and any configured generation providers may retain information under their own policies. This automation is not used to collect data from other Instagram users.

Choices and requests

The account owner can disconnect the app through Meta’s settings and stop or delete workflow data in n8n. Contact Meta or a configured provider directly about information they hold. The owner can remove published content through Instagram.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the automation’s data use changes. For questions about this personal automation, contact the account owner through the public Instagram profile @vibehaze_english.
